Dairy cattle types documented on the Coast

Coastal Kenya dairy research has discussed Ayrshire, Guernsey, Jersey, Brown Swiss, and Sahiwal cattle, as well as crossbreds. Kwale has also distributed hybrid dairy cows. These records show regional use or study, not today's seller inventory. Ask your county livestock office which animals are currently available and suited to your farm.

Choose a cow that fits your farm

  1. Estimate the fodder, clean water, housing, and daily labour you can provide.
  2. Ask a local livestock officer which breed or cross suits your climate and feed supply.
  3. Ask a trusted seller for health, breeding, and milk-production records.
  4. Have a veterinarian or livestock officer examine the animal before purchase.
  5. Plan the market for your milk and the costs of feed, treatment, and transport.

Check availability

Availability changes. Contact your county livestock office or a trusted local breeder to confirm current stock, breed or cross, age, price, and health records before travelling.
