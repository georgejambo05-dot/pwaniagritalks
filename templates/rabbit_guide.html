Rabbit types reported in the Coastal region

A University of Nairobi rabbit study included Taita Taveta in its Coastal-region sample. It recorded New Zealand White, Californian White, and crossbred rabbits among the breeds kept in its study. The published summary does not give a separate current stock count for each coastal county, so contact a local breeder or county livestock office before travelling.

New Zealand White

A white meat-production breed reported in Kenyan rabbit farming.

Californian White

Usually white with darker ears, nose, feet, and tail.

Crossbred rabbits

Crossbred rabbits do not all look the same. Their appearance depends on their parent breeds. Ask the breeder which breeds were crossed.

Crossbreds were also recorded in the study. Ask to see the parent animals and health records.

Before you start

  1. Find out whether local buyers want rabbit meat or breeding stock before starting a large herd.
  2. Prepare clean, dry, shaded housing with airflow and protection from predators and rain.
  3. Plan for suitable feed, clean water, and daily cleaning.
  4. Start with a few healthy rabbits from a known breeder. Ask about age, parent breeds, and health history.
  5. Ask a livestock officer or veterinarian about local disease prevention and safe breeding.
