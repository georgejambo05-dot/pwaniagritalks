Improved and commercial poultry types

The Kenya Livestock Breeds Catalogue lists Kuroiler, Rainbow Rooster, Sasso, and Kenbro among improved chicken types distributed in Kenya, including the Coastal region. Commercial hatcheries also sell broiler chicks for meat and layer chicks for eggs. These names describe different strains and production goals; a bird’s exact performance depends on its source, feed, housing, and care.

Pictures below are examples of each type. Always confirm the strain and source with the hatchery before ordering.

Broiler chickens

Meat-focused birds. They need close attention to clean water, ventilation, feed, and temperature, especially when young.

Commercial layers

Egg-focused birds. Ask the hatchery for the exact layer strain, vaccination record, and feeding plan.

Kenbro

An improved dual-purpose type kept for meat and eggs. Confirm the exact line and management advice with the supplier.

Kuroiler

An improved dual-purpose type. Ask the seller whether the chicks are genuine Kuroiler and what vaccination they have received.

Sasso

A coloured improved type. Sasso offers different lines, so ask which line the supplier is selling and what it is bred to produce.

Rainbow Rooster

An improved dual-purpose type. Confirm whether the chicks are Rainbow Rooster or Rainbow Rooster Plus and ask about current stock.

How to choose

  1. Choose your goal first: meat, eggs, or both. Broilers suit meat production; layers suit egg production; the improved types are commonly kept for both.
  2. Compare the full cost of chicks, feed, housing, vaccines, transport, and labour before buying.
  3. Prepare a dry, airy, secure house with shade, clean bedding, and enough space for the flock.
  4. Use clean water and the feed designed for the bird's age and type. Ask a livestock officer for a vaccination and health plan for your area.
  5. Buy from a reputable hatchery or approved seller. Get the breed/strain, hatch date, vaccination details, and receipt in writing.

Check local availability

The catalogue reports these improved chicken types across the Coastal region, but does not provide a live count of chicks available in each county. Contact a hatchery or county livestock office to confirm the exact strain, delivery to your area, price, and vaccination record before paying.
