Getting started

  1. Decide whether your main goal is meat, milk, or both.
  2. Prepare a dry, secure shelter with airflow, shade, and protection from rain and predators.
  3. Plan daily access to clean water and suitable fodder.
  4. Buy from a known breeder. Ask the age, parentage, vaccination and treatment history, and any milk records.
  5. Ask a veterinarian or county livestock officer about local disease prevention and breeding.

Check coastal availability

Kwale has documented Galla and hybrid Galla distribution, but that does not mean animals are in stock everywhere. Contact your county livestock office or a local breeder to confirm current stock, parentage, age, price, and health history.
